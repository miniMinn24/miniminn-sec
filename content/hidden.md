## Research

Animations often provide a "safe space" for emotional regulation that live-action media cannot. The stylized, non-realistic nature of characters reduces the cognitive load required to process social cues, creating a low-risk environment for emotional engagement. Research indicates that watching wholesome cartoons lowers cortisol levels and releases endorphins, offering a genuine physiological stress relief. For individuals who have experienced difficult social histories, these shows offer predictability and comfort; the narratives usually resolve positively, reinforcing a sense of order and safety that may have been missing in your personal experiences.
